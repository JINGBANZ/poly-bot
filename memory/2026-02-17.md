### Key Lessons
1. **Crypto threshold = best strategy** — mechanical resolution (Binance candle), calculable fair value, no subjective judgment
2. **Stop losses too tight = death** — 3 of 4 losses were SL triggers. Wider stops needed for positions with real edge.
3. **Sports betting edge is hard** — bookmaker comparison works for esports (FURIA) but soccer/basketball edges are often illusory
4. **Binance API blocked from our server** — use CoinGecko instead for price data
5. **Polymarket data-api endpoints**: `trades?user=`, `positions?user=`, `activity?user=` all work for whale tracking

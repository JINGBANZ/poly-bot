## The Rules (v5)

### Entry Rules
1. **Value zone only: 10-45¢.** Never above 50¢. Asymmetric payoffs required.
2. **Must have WRITTEN thesis before placing trade.** If you can't write 3 sentences of evidence, don't trade.
3. **Max $2 per position.** With $20 total capital, diversification > concentration.
4. **Liquidity check: >$50K 24h volume.** Dead orderbooks = trapped capital. This is NON-NEGOTIABLE. Learned from Italy Gold, Google AI, Sean Penn, EB — all became exit traps.

### Edge Categories (ranked by historical performance)
1. **Earnings beats** (best edge) — Only when consensus clearly exceeds threshold AND beat rate >70%. OXY was our best trade.
2. **AI/Tech benchmarks** — Verifiable data (LMSYS leaderboard). Google AI position is strong.
3. **Geopolitics at cheap prices** — Israel/Iran at 27¢ is asymmetric. Don't need to be right often.
4. **Cultural events (Oscars, etc.)** — Diversified lottery tickets at <20¢. Expert consensus is public and useful.
5. **Crypto thresholds** — Mixed results. Need better vol modeling.
6. **Sports** — AVOID unless verified insider edge. Our worst category by far.

### Portfolio Rules
- Target 8-12 positions diversified across categories
- No more than 30% of portfolio in any single category
- Keep 25% cash reserve for opportunities
- Review all positions weekly

### Exit Rules
- **No stop-losses under 20¢ on small positions.** Just let lottery tickets ride.
- **Sell when thesis changes** (like HHH — consensus dropped below threshold)
- **Take profit at 2x entry or higher** if no catalyst remaining
- **Never hold through resolution if edge has evaporated**

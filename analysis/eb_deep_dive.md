## Q4 2024 Actual Results (reported Feb 27, 2025)

- **Revenue:** $76.5M (at upper end of $74-77M guidance, slightly beat consensus of $75.67M)
- **GAAP EPS:** -$0.09 (**missed** consensus of -$0.05)
- **Revenue down 13% YoY** (vs Q4 2023's $87.8M)
- Paid ticket volume: 21.6M
- Consumer reach: 87M+ MAU
- **Stock dropped** on the EPS miss
- 2025 guidance: lower revenue range than 2024

## Full Year 2024
